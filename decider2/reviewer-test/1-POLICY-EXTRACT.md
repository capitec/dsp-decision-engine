# Credit Policy — Personal Loans
## Extract: Section 7.4, Maximum Term

*Approved by the Credit Policy Committee, 14 August 2026. Supersedes the version
of 3 February 2026. Next scheduled review: February 2027.*

---

**7.4.1** The maximum contractual term for a personal loan is **60 months**.

**7.4.2** Where the applicant's **verified net monthly income** is below
**R8 000**, the maximum contractual term is reduced to **48 months**.

**7.4.3** Where the applicant is employed on a fixed-term contract expiring
within the proposed term, the maximum contractual term is reduced to the lesser
of 48 months and the number of whole months remaining on the contract.

**7.4.4** The reductions in 7.4.2 and 7.4.3 apply cumulatively with any other
term reduction arising elsewhere in this policy. Where two or more reductions
apply, **the shortest resulting term governs**.

**7.4.5** No term reduction under this section may be waived, overridden or
extended by a pricing, campaign or affordability adjustment. Term reductions
under 7.4.2 and 7.4.3 are **floors on risk**, not pricing inputs.

---

*Note for reviewers: "verified net monthly income" is defined in Section 3.2 as
income after statutory deductions, evidenced by payslip or bank statement
affordability assessment. It is not gross income and it is not declared income.*
