# Scope: one vertical slice per spec, for one implementer session each

Rules applied to every slice:

1. **Keep the dominant difficulty** from README §4 at real size wherever the size *is* the difficulty.
   Generating a 63 360-cell grid is cheap, so generate large tables synthetically at their full dimensions. Do not shrink them.
2. **Keep the parts later projects consume** (see DEPS.md), with their published names and the 00-ADDENDUM fixes.
3. **Cut breadth.** Build fewer products, rules, stages and entry points, but make each one end to end: inputs, logic, evidence record, overlay on/off, replay against `decision_date`, and one change scenario from §11.
4. **Every slice meets the 09-C evidence minimum** (09 §5.15): decision id, stable logic ids, table version and cell, overlay stack with unadjusted values, reason codes with registry version, and no "today".

**00: shared core library (686 lines).** Build the vocabulary from §4 with the addendum A1–A8 names, and the library-wide mechanisms: `core.dates` (effective-dated resolution), `core.reason_codes` (registry with ranking and primary reason), `core.adjustments` (register, scope, declared order, tighten-only check at definition, expiry and lapse, stack-off run), `core.rounding`, and table version/cell attribution with a diff artefact. On top of those, build the capabilities consumers call in waves 1–2 at working depth: `core.instalment` with its inverse, `core.fees`, `core.rate_card` (the Flex Loan card at its full 96×55×12 size, generated, returning a structured result with a cell id), `core.scorecard` (per-characteristic contributions), `core.calibration`, `core.risk_grade`, and the five affordability units `core.income`, `core.deductions`, `core.expense_norms`, `core.obligations` and `core.affordability`. `core.obligations` must return both the scalar and the per-account annotation. Build these five as units only; 02 builds the assessment on top of them. For `core.bureau` (one bureau schema), `core.eligibility`, `core.appetite`, `core.exposure` (no graph), `core.consent`, `core.credit_life` and `core.adverse_events` (all 14 types, thresholds supplied by the caller), deliver the frozen interface with a thin, correct implementation. Prove §10 items 4 (same capability twice with different settings), 6 (re-derive to the cent) and 8 (stack off). Skip two live majors (§7.1), the four bureau formats and the spreadsheet-driven policy tests.

**01: transaction fraud (1 179 lines).** Rule volume is the difficulty, so keep it at volume. Build a generated rule set of about 520 live rules and 100 shadow rules across the 5 families, with the full per-rule attribute set from §6.1. Build the fired-set capture (every live and shadow firing, and which firings happened only because of an overlay), action resolution by severity and priority (§5.12), and reason ranking through `core.reason_codes`. Include 3 of the 7 overlay kinds from §6.5 (a sensitivity dial, a threshold multiplier and action escalation), including overlay-exempt rules and auto-lapse. Build backtest mode (§5.17) that shares the rule definitions and is proven identical to live over a stored event sample. Cover one event type family (instant payments) and stub enrichment and the velocity store with the degradation codes from §5.6. Skip dispatch, feedback (§5.16), the client-wording languages and the latency target. Test one latency point at 900 rules (§10 item 15).

**02: affordability (983 lines).** Build all seven stages for single and joint applicants, on top of 00's five units. Include the §5.2 evidence waterfall, the §5.5 obligations dedup across two sources with all ten treatment behaviours, the §5.6.2 buffer with tighten-only overlays, and the §5.7 verdict. Build the **four modes of §5.8** sharing one arithmetic path, and the three answer shapes of §5.7.2, including the monotonicity guarantee 03 relies on. The five consumers are the reason this project exists, so include one acceptance test per consuming mode and a 400-call scenario loop (06's usage pattern) under budget. Skip the full evidence ladder rendering (§9.1) and variable-pay edge cases beyond one component.

**03: unsecured granting and pricing (1 411 lines).** Build for Flex Loan (product 10) only. Include: eligibility gates, the fraud verdict (stubbed with the 03 §4.4 contract), one scorecard with calibration and grading plus the §5.4.1 overlays, the **cap waterfall** at real volume (all 52 rules, three ceilings, the full chain recorded), affordability consumed from 02, pricing through the library's full-size generated card, and **the solve** (§5.8). The solve must include the non-monotone band-edge case as a named test, the evaluation ceiling, the tie-break and the binding constraint. It must also be published so that 06 can call it. Build offer-set construction and final validation, and add batch mode over 100 k records showing identical output to real-time (§10 item 11), because 04 consumes it. Skip disclosure outputs (§5.11) and the 14 M-record timing.

**04: campaign targeting trees (1 212 lines).** The difficulty is trees and paths, so keep the tree count and size near real: generate about 60 trees, 20–400 nodes each. Build: tree authoring from a data definition, with the validation of §5.9 (unreachable or contradictory nodes); evaluation with full path capture (§5.4.1); the **node identity map across two tree versions** (§5.4.3, §10 item 7); overlays over published trees (volume dial, cut-off shift, cap reduction that may only reduce) with the unadjusted leaf recorded; suppressions; and a simple arbitration. Consume 03 batch pre-assessment, or a stub with the §5.5 "Emits" contract. Run over a synthetic population of about 1 M rows and extrapolate to 14.2 M. Skip holdout and challenger design beyond deterministic hashing (§5.7), plus dispatch and fairness reporting.

**05: business nested entities (1 652 lines).** Nesting is the difficulty, so keep it at full depth. Build: structure resolution (§5.1, a natural person reached by two paths is evaluated once), per-entity criticality, **adverse event classification** through `core.adverse_events` with criticality-dependent thresholds and an overlay at stack position 1, the entity adverse verdict roll-up (a subset of about 6 of the 12 rules, including one count rule that attributes to a set of events), entity scoring for one family, the people blend, and the combined grade with a reduced overlay stack. Attribution must propagate to the business decline (§10 items 1–3, including invariance under shuffling), and a single-event counterfactual must be available. For sole proprietors, call 02 in the regulated regime. Take structuring and pricing only as far as a single product 50 lookup. Skip financial spreading beyond 3 ratios, and skip the committee pack. Build the partial one-entity re-assessment with an equivalence test, because 11 depends on it (11 §5.17.5).

**06: consolidation and restructure (1 391 lines).** The search is the difficulty. **07: credit limit management (1 348 lines).** The portfolio constraint is the difficulty.
